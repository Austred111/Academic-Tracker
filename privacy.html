Privacy Policy

Academic Tracker · Last updated November 6, 2026

Academic Tracker keeps your data on your own device and, only if you choose to connect it, in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Information the app stores

  • Your homework and events: subjects, titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions, page numbers, sources, group member names you type in, progress and favourites. These are stored on your device.
  • Your settings: your weekly class schedule, colour theme, subject acronyms and colours, and event category settings.
  • A Google sign-in token: stored on your device only, and only if you connect Google Drive. It lets the app keep syncing without asking you to sign in again.
  • The app has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Google user data

  • Optional: connecting Google Drive is optional. The app works fully without it.
  • Limited permission: the app requests only the drive.file permission. It can only access the "AcademicTracker" folder and the files it created inside it. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • How it is used: only to save copies of your homework, events, schedule and settings to that folder and keep them the same on all your devices.
  • Where it goes: your data travels directly between your device and Google. It is never sent to the developer or any other server.
  • No sharing or selling: your Google data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Academic Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Log out in the app: Settings → GDrive-Sync → Log out. This removes the sign-in from that device. Your homework and events stay on the device.
  • Remove the app's access: at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the synced copies: delete the "AcademicTracker" folder from your Google Drive.
  • Delete everything on a device: uninstall the app, or clear its storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
