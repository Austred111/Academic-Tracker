Academic Tracker

A personal homework, events and class-schedule app for Android and Windows.

What it does

  • Homework: track each assignment's subject, start and due dates, pages, source, description and progress.
  • Events: keep track of exams and other events with dates, times, location and an Outdoors / Indoors / Hybrid category.
  • Ongoing and Finished: finished work moves to its own list automatically and can be restored later.
  • Group work: list the members of a group task or event.
  • Weekly schedule: a Monday–Friday class board that highlights today.
  • Your own look: custom colour themes, subject colours and favourites, with sorting and filtering.

Syncing with Google Drive

  • Connecting a Google account is optional. It keeps your homework, events, schedule and settings the same on all your devices by saving them in an "AcademicTracker" folder in your own Google Drive.
  • The app requests only the drive.file permission, so it can only access files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • Without Google Drive, the app works normally and keeps your data only on your device.
